## 2. About Kultivate

Kultivate is a digital agency that helps businesses build and develop their digital presence through Website Development, Search Engine Optimization (SEO), and Social Media Management. Each service may stand alone or operate as part of a connected digital growth system.

### Website Development - Foundation

Build a clear, responsive, performant, accessible, and extensible website system that becomes the center of information, credibility, and conversion.

### SEO - Discoverability

Strengthen technical foundations, information architecture, search intent alignment, content structure, on-page optimization, measurement, and iteration.

### Social Media Management - Distribution

Build a consistent content, communication, distribution, publishing, and reporting system according to the agreed scope.

### Pending public facts

Do not publish until approved:

- public geographic coverage;
- founding story and milestones;
- final team structure and profiles;
- legal/public contact details;
- client proof, testimonials, and outcomes.

## 3. Purpose, Vision, Mission, and Values

### Purpose

Make digital growth more connected and easier to understand.

### Vision

Become a digital partner that turns separate channels into a directed growth system.

### Mission

Build the foundation, improve discoverability, and maintain distribution through clear scope, purposeful craft, inspectable evidence, and continuous optimization.

### Values

#### 1. Clarity

Explain objective, scope, deliverables, dependencies, decisions, and next steps in understandable language.

#### 2. Connected Thinking

Connect website, SEO, and social media through shared messaging, content architecture, analytics, and next action.

#### 3. Craft with Purpose

Visual and interaction decisions must improve hierarchy, trust, accessibility, and conversion.

#### 4. Evidence over Hype

Use verified data, audit findings, process proof, and real deliverables. Avoid guarantees of ranking, virality, engagement, traffic, or sales.

#### 5. Continuous Cultivation

Launch is not the finish. The system is maintained, measured, learned from, and improved.

#### 6. Calm Confidence

Use restraint, generous spacing, and direct language. Do not use excessive glow, motion, density, or jargon to appear capable.

## 5. Tone of Voice

### Character

- Professional, not stiff.
- Clear, not over-detailed.
- Confident, not loud.
- Specific, not generic.
- Modern, not trend-chasing.
- Calm, not passive.

### Writing rules

- Use one central idea per section.
- Use active sentences and concrete verbs.
- Lead with benefit or decision, then show proof.
- Keep homepage copy concise.
- Move detail to service, work, and insight pages.
- Retain English technical terms when they are more precise.
- Never make a recommendation sound like a guaranteed outcome.

### Copy density targets

- Hero supporting copy: 24-32 words.
- Section intro: 20-40 words.
- Card body: 18-24 words.
- CTA support: one sentence.
- Homepage paragraph: generally no more than three desktop lines.

### Avoid

- best, number one, guaranteed, viral, award-winning, or similar unsupported claims;
- jargon without explanation;
- long tool lists on the homepage;
- generic agency copy;
- dense paragraphs or tiny text used to fit more content.

## 7. Visual Direction

### Direction name

**Dark Editorial**

### Description

A black-dominant editorial system using restrained blue-cyan illumination, large typography, balanced asymmetry, modular composition, and generous negative space. Growth is expressed through relationships, light, and controlled transformation rather than literal plant imagery.

### Core cues

- Dark field.
- Light as material.
- Modular editorial.
- Digital organic.

### Do

- Use black backgrounds and localized glow.
- Use generous negative space and fewer words.
- Use editorial blocks and balanced asymmetry.
- Use selected work visuals as proof.
- Let typography carry a meaningful part of the communication.

### Do not

- Make the website overly bright.
- Create an overly dense dashboard or bento wall.
- Depend on decorative effects for quality.
- Copy reference compositions directly.
- Use literal leaves, sprouts, or generic growth icons.
- Use neon rainbow, excessive glows, or decorative 3D objects.

### Recommended proportion

- Void Black / Deep Navy: 60%.
- Cloud White / Mist: 20%.
- Electric Blue: 12%.
- Cyan Glow / Signal Ice: 6%.
- Functional accents: 2%.

The final composition must still visually read as black-dominant. The proportion is a system guide, not permission to fill large areas with bright blue.

### Usage rules

- Use Electric Blue for primary CTA and active state.
- Use Cyan Glow only as accent, glow, line, or focal illumination.
- Use one dominant glow per composition.
- Keep long text Cloud White or Mist on dark backgrounds.
- Do not use bright blue as the default full-section background.
- Functional color must be supported by an icon, label, or message.
- Verify final contrast on actual backgrounds and imagery.

## 10. Typography

### Display accent

**Sounds Right**

Use selectively for hero accents and a small number of large editorial headings. Never use it for body copy, navigation, buttons, or dense UI. The exact production WOFF2/WOFF2 variable file and usage permission are pending; until supplied, the site must fall back to Instrument Sans.

### Primary typeface

**Instrument Sans**

Use for headings, body, navigation, buttons, captions, and UI labels. It is also the approved fallback for every Sounds Right role.

### Supporting typeface

**Space Mono**

Use only for short metadata, index numbers, dates, status labels, technical annotations, and numeric callouts.

### Rules

- Use short, strong headings with clear hierarchy.
- Keep body line length around 55-75 characters.
- Use uppercase only for short labels and metadata.
- Use responsive sizing with CSS `clamp()`.
- Do not use a condensed primary typeface.
- Do not squeeze large type into narrow columns.
- Keep Sounds Right selective and display-only; keep Space Mono secondary to Instrument Sans.

## 11. Imagery

Photography must show real people, work, and systems - not generic corporate stock.

### Subjects

- founder and team;
- client interaction;
- workshop and research;
- interface and content production;
- approved project visuals.

### Direction

- Directional and controlled lighting.
- Editorial crop with useful negative space.
- Layered depth and intentional asymmetry.
- Natural skin tone or monochrome treatment.
- Blue used as a lighting mood, not as a filter that hides reality.

### Avoid

- handshake stock photos;
- fake glow;
- heavy blur that hides the subject;
- extreme cyan skin tone;
- unapproved client, office, dashboard, or project material.

## 12. Graphic Elements

### Connected nodes

Show relationships between service, content, data, and next action. Keep the number of nodes limited and meaningful.

### Light field

Use one dominant blue-cyan glow to create depth, focus, or transition.

### Organic curve

Use soft curves to imply cultivation and connection without literal plant symbols.

### Expanding grid

Use grid to express scale, information architecture, and structure. The grid must follow the layout.

### Modular block

Use blocks only when content benefits from grouping, such as services, deliverables, case studies, or metadata.

### Technical line

Use hairlines, indexes, or annotations for precision. Do not turn the interface into a sci-fi control panel.

### Iconography

- Outline icons as default.
- Light stroke with moderate rounded joins.
- Clear at small sizes.
- Secondary to text labels.
- Filled state only when functional.

## 13. Layout and Grid

### Grid baseline

- Desktop: 12 columns.
- Tablet: 8 columns.
- Mobile: 4 columns.
- Maximum width: 1280-1440 px.
- Base spacing unit: 8 px.
- Section spacing: 96-160 px depending on importance.
- Radius: 12-24 px.
- Border: 1 px, subtle.

### Rules

- One main job per section.
- Asymmetry happens inside the grid, not outside it.
- Negative space must improve hierarchy.
- Use wider content containers than the earlier condensed concept.
- Card size follows importance and content depth.
- Mobile is redesigned by priority, not simply stacked.

### Anti-density rules

- Do not place more than three equal cards in one row.
- Do not shrink copy to fit more content.
- Do not build every section as a bento grid.
- Do not show more than one dominant visual story per major section.
- Do not fill empty space with decorative metadata.

## 14. Motion and UI

Motion clarifies state and interaction. It is not a substitute for content or proof.

### Selected approach

**Static-first.**

- No heavy WebGL.
- No scroll hijacking.
- No constant background animation.
- No effect that hides critical content.

### Timing

| Type | Duration | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Micro interaction | 150-250 ms | CTA, focus, icon, tooltip. |
| Image/card | 300-600 ms | Reveal, scale, or crossfade. |
| Section transition | 500-800 ms | Limited stagger and directional movement. |

### Allowed

- button state change;
- underline or arrow movement;
- focus transition;
- card border emphasis;
- menu open/close;
- language-switch state;
- restrained reveal only when it improves hierarchy;
- left-to-right service image reveal on hover or keyboard focus, with text readable before and during motion;
- three-up Insights layout without overflow on desktop; user-controlled scroll-snap carousel below 900 px without autoplay;
- slow transform-only decorative motion in Insights visuals with a reduced-motion fallback.

### Non-negotiables

- Implement `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Critical content must remain available without animation.
- Keyboard navigation and visible focus are mandatory.
- Use responsive images, modern formats, and lazy loading below the fold.
- Keep the interface calm and performance-friendly.

## 16. Accessibility and Content Integrity

### Accessibility

- Semantic landmarks and heading order.
- Keyboard-accessible navigation.
- Visible focus.
- Skip-to-content link.
- Meaningful alt text and empty alt for decorative images.
- Form labels, descriptions, errors, and success states.
- No color-only status communication.
- Correct page language attribute.
- Reduced-motion support.

### Content integrity

Do not publish without verification and usage permission:

- project/client names and logos;
- testimonial;
- dashboard screenshot;
- performance metric;
- award or credential;
- team profile or photography;
- legal and contact facts.

Every metric must include baseline, period, method, and source.
