Search visibility brings customers to the page. The page still needs to explain what you offer, why it is relevant, and what they can do next.

## Continue the question

The page title and opening should continue the search or content that brought the customer there. Keep the same subject and level of detail so the answer is easy to recognize.

## Keep the decision simple

Present the service, relevant proof, and next step in a clear order. Customers should not need the full company story before they can understand the answer they came for.

## Make the next step clear

Use a specific call to action, a focused form, and honest information about what happens after an inquiry is sent.
