[Heading 1] How to use this prompt
[List Bullet] Run it one lesson at a time for the highest quality. Provide the course/lesson input block from the planning document.
[List Bullet] The AI must research current sources before drafting any technical/search claim that can change over time.
[List Bullet] Use Indonesian as the primary script language with English technical terms where clearer. Prepare English adaptation only after the Indonesian version is approved.
[List Bullet] Do not ask the AI to imitate Alex Hormozi or Neil Patel. The prompt explicitly borrows only principles: clarity, frameworks, examples, evidence, and a natural free-value-to-consultation bridge.

[Heading 1] Non-negotiable brand guardrails
[List Bullet] Kultivate remains the brand. Hans is the recognizable expert/teacher, not a separate influencer business.
[List Bullet] Accessible for non-technical founders, but never oversimplify into incorrect SEO/AEO/GEO claims.
[List Bullet] No aggressive urgency, artificial scarcity, borrowed status, or imitation of creator personality.
[List Bullet] No fabricated proof for “proof of concept”. Placeholder UI must be explicitly labeled as sample/hypothetical.
[List Bullet] Do not claim a channel is always the answer. Good authority includes the ability to say “this is not the priority yet.”
[List Bullet] The consultation is free because it is contextual diagnosis; paid value starts at scoped research/strategy/execution/implementation/QA/ongoing optimization.
