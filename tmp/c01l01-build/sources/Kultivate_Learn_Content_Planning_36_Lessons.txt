[Heading 1] Planning principles
[List Bullet] Primary audience: CEO/Owner, Marketing, IT Lead, Procurement. Indonesia primary; English secondary.
[List Bullet] Primary objective: build trust and problem awareness, then move the right learner toward Growth Roadmap and a free first consultation within roughly one month when intent exists.
[List Bullet] One lesson = one decision or mental model. Default video duration 6–12 minutes.
[List Bullet] Free: problem, framework, high-level diagnostic, trade-offs, “what good looks like”, worksheet/checklist. Paid/service boundary: contextual research, prioritization, implementation, QA, stakeholder coordination, ongoing measurement.
[List Bullet] Proof policy: no fabricated Kultivate results. Use official sources, verified public cases, public-site teardown, or clearly labeled hypothetical examples until real client evidence exists.

[Heading 1] Qualification logic
[TABLE]
Signal | Consult now | Nurture | Filter / expectation reset
Intent | Planning / comparing / ready to execute | Learning only | Wants guaranteed outcome or tactics outside approved/ethical scope
Timeline | <1 month or 1–3 months | 3+ months / no timeline | Impossible deadline with no trade-off
Context | Can share URL, goal, current problem, relevant constraints | Not enough context yet | Refuses minimum context but expects custom diagnosis
Role | Decision-maker or meaningful stakeholder | Influencer/learner not yet aligned internally | No authority and only seeks free implementation
Service fit | SEO/AEO/GEO, website strategy/UX/technical/migration within Kultivate scope | Potential future fit | Outside service scope / black-hat / unsupported guarantee
