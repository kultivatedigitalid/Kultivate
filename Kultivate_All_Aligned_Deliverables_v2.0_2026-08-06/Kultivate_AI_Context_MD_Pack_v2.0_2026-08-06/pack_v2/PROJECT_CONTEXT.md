## Brand

Kultivate (PT Karya Lintas Generasi) is a digital agency offering Website Development, SEO, and Social Media Management. The strategic message connects the services as one digital growth system.

## Brand foundation

- Essence: Connected Digital Growth.
- Promise: Clarity before complexity.
- Personality: Professional, Modern, Creative, Calm.
- Primary audience: founders, business owners, Marketing Leads, Brand Leads, and internal stakeholders at growing businesses in Indonesia.
- Primary CTA: Mulai Proyek / Start a Project.

## Primary website jobs

1. Generate qualified inquiry.
2. Build credibility through real work, process, deliverables, and verified proof.
3. Explain the connected service system and support organic discovery.
